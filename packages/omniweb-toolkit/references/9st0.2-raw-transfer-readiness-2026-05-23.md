# 9st0.2 Raw Transfer Readiness Audit

Status: `TRANSFER_INTEGER_READY`

Bead: `omniweb-agents-9st0.2`

Date: `2026-05-23`

## Scope

This audit performed no live spend, no broadcast, no upload, no mainnet action,
and no credential or profile mutation.

The goal was to keep the successor raw-transfer lane honest after `sc96`:
integer DEM preview remains satisfiable, while fractional DEM stays explicitly
blocked unless decimal-to-base-unit conversion is proven.

## Findings

- Runtime amount support is centralized in `classifyDemTransferAmount()`.
- Invalid, zero, negative, and non-finite amounts fail closed before transfer.
- Non-integer DEM amounts fail closed before SDK confirmation with an explicit
  reason: Demos confirms only integer DEM amounts and no base-unit conversion is
  proven.
- `safeTransfer()` calls the same classifier before invoking the supplied
  executor, so fractional DEM cannot reach the transfer implementation through
  the safe transfer helper.
- `probe-chain-transfer.ts` defaults to `1 DEM`, caps transfer proofs at
  `5 DEM`, records `amountSupport`, and records a `unitContract` with
  `supportedUnit: integer-dem`, `baseUnitConversion: not_proven`, and
  `fractionalAmounts: unsupported`.
- Existing no-spend artifact
  `sc96-successor-readiness-2026-05-23/transfer-preview.json` remains
  `PREVIEW_GREEN` for `1 DEM` with sender and recipient balance readback.
- The old `0ctx.8` `0.1 DEM` raw-transfer attempt remains historical evidence
  of an unsupported fractional amount, not a live template. The 0ctx packet now
  states that its `0.1 DEM` cap is superseded by the integer-only successor
  contract.

## Public Surface Check

Current public/runtime surfaces that describe raw transfer support now agree:

- `src/toolkit/sdk-bridge.ts`: `transferDem()` documents and enforces integer
  DEM only.
- `packages/omniweb-toolkit/src/chain-api.ts`: `omni.chain.transfer()` returns
  the classifier reason for fractional amounts before calling `transferDem()`.
- `packages/omniweb-toolkit/references/toolkit-guardrails.md`: raw
  `transferDem()` requires integer DEM until base-unit conversion is proven.
- `packages/omniweb-toolkit/src/capability-manifest.ts`: `omni.chain.transfer`
  notes the integer-only raw DEM transfer contract.
- `docs/goalmode/successor-unblock-runway-2026-05-23.md`: successor exit
  criteria require integer preview plus explicit fractional blocking or proven
  base-unit conversion.

Historical references may still mention `0.1 DEM` for escrow, betting, or old
raw-transfer evidence. Those are not current raw-transfer support claims unless
they explicitly prove a base-unit conversion path.

## Successor Recommendation

For `omniweb-agents-9st0.9`, consume raw transfer as:

- status: `TRANSFER_INTEGER_READY`
- supported preview amount: integer DEM, with `1 DEM` as the smallest current
  no-spend proof budget
- fractional DEM: `BLOCKED_UNTIL_BASE_UNIT_CONVERSION_PROVEN`
- live authority: none from this audit

Any future fractional support must first add a tested decimal-to-base-unit
conversion contract, prove the SDK/runtime accepts that base-unit payload, update
the public capability docs, and rerun a no-spend preview before any live packet.
